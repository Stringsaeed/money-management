# Z2 Hyperdrive vs Z1 D1 disposable-stage latency

Both series ran from the same host through public `commands.apply`, 20 warm sequential `transaction.create` calls per stage. They ran consecutively because each disposable stage had to be destroyed before the next lifecycle. Ingress differed: Z2 was observed at NRT and Z1 at HKG.

| Series | Median | p90 | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Z1 `d1661b9`, D1 | 1,991.5 ms | 2,040 ms | 20/20 applied |
| Z2 `8640e41`, Hyperdrive + PlanetScale | 554.5 ms | 620 ms | 20/20 applied |

Z2 median was 72.2% lower than Z1. The Z1 +40% ceiling is 2,788.1 ms, so Z2 passes the relative median rule. Z2 also passes the absolute p90 rule of less than 1 second.
