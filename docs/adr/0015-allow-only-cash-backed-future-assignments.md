# Allow only cash-backed Future Assignments

Users may fund future Budget Periods only from Unassigned Money they already own, and that Money becomes unavailable for any other Assignment immediately. Future Assignments are unavailable while the currency workspace has cash Envelope Overspending or Unfunded Card Spending, and any expected Rollover remains visibly projected until its period begins. This supports planning ahead without bypassing current obligations, counting expected income, or duplicating the same Funding Pool across monthly budgets.
