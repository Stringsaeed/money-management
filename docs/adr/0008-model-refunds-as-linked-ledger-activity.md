# Model Refunds as linked ledger activity

One expense may have multiple same-currency partial Refunds whose cumulative amount cannot exceed the original expense. Card Refunds return to the original card, while cash-account Refunds may enter any same-currency Funding Account; each Refund restores the original Envelope in the Refund's Budget Period and adjusts any Card Payment Reserve without erasing purchase history. Safe corrections recalculate linked effects, but changes that would make the original expense incompatible with its Refunds and deletion before reversing those Refunds are blocked. Treating Refunds as unrelated income would distort reports, retroactively rewriting the purchase period would misstate when Money returned, and editing or deleting the purchase would conceal what actually happened.
