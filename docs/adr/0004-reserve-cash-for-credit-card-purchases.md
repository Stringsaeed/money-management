# Reserve cash for credit-card purchases

Credit-card Accounts do not contribute to Funding Pools, but a categorized card purchase moves supported Money from its Envelope into a system-managed Card Payment Reserve. Any purchase amount that the Envelope cannot support remains explicit Unfunded Card Spending rather than creating imaginary reserved cash. Later Assignments cover cash Envelope Overspending first, then the oldest Unfunded Card Spending, and only then create new Envelope availability.
